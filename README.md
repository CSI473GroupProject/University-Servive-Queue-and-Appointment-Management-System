# University of Botswana Service Queue and Appointment Management System

## Problem Statement
Students at the University of Botswana often experience long waiting times when accessing university services. The lack of an organized queue and appointment system can lead to overcrowding, inefficient service delivery, and difficulty managing student appointments.

## Goal
To develop a digital system that manages student queues and appointments efficiently, reducing waiting times and improving service delivery at the University of Botswana.

## Objectives
- Allow students to book appointments for university services.
- Allow students to join and manage virtual queues.
- Provide staff with an organized way to manage appointments and queues.
- Reduce waiting times and overcrowding.
- Provide students with updates about their queue or appointment status.

## Stakeholders
- University of Botswana Students
- University Staff
- Service Centre Staff
- University Management
- System Administrators
